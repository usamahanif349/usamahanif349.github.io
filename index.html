Pakistan AQI Prediction

How bad will the air be in Lahore, Karachi or Peshawar next month?

I compared classical ML, a neural network and an LSTM on 10 years of monthly data for 10 cities. Gradient boosting won, and SHAP shows smog season and city drive most of the prediction. Written up as a research paper.

Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow, SHAP, Streamlit
